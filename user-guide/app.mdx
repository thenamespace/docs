Activate your ENS name in the [Namespace App](https://app.namespace.ninja) to let people mint subnames under it on Ethereum (L1). Each subname is minted onchain and owned by the wallet that mints it. You set the price, the rules, and who can mint, then confirm in your wallet.

New activations need an ENSv2 name. Listings on ENSv1 names keep working and can still be edited, but they stay on ENSv1. Pick the tab that matches your name.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="ENSv2">
    ## Before you begin

    - Connect the wallet that owns the parent ENS name.
    - Pick a name tagged `v2`. The name picker greys out ENSv1 names.
    - Keep some ETH in the wallet to pay network fees for the activation transaction.
    - Decide on a base price and how payouts should be split.

    ## Activate your ENS name

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select your name">
        In the sidebar, open **Onchain subnames** and then **ENSv2**. Pick the name from **Search your names**.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/activate-select-name.png" alt="Onchain subnames page with the name picker open, listing an ENSv1 name tagged v1 and onchin.eth tagged v2" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Start the activation">
        The page shows **Name not activated** for a name without a listing. Click **Activate**.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/activate-name-not-activated.png" alt="onchin.eth selected with a Name not activated message and the Activate button" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Choose Mainnet subnames">
        Select **Mainnet subnames**, which places subnames on Ethereum in the ENSv2 registry. Click **Next**.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/activate-choose-mainnet.png" alt="Where should subnames of onchin.eth live, with Mainnet subnames selected and the Next button" />

      </Step>

      <Step title="Set up expiration, base price, and payout">
        On the **Setup** tab, choose an expiration mode:

        - **Permanent**: subnames are paid for once.
        - **Expirable**: subnames are rented for a chosen duration and renewed to keep. The base price is charged per year.

        Enter the **Base price** in dollars. It applies when no other price rule matches. Minters pay in USDC or DAI. Under **Payout**, keep **100% to owner** or choose **Split payment**.

        Type a label into **Price check** on the right to see what it would cost under your current rules.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/configure-setup.png" alt="Setup tab with Expirable selected, a base price of 1 dollar per year, 100% to owner payout, and the Price check panel showing 1 dollar per year for alice" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add optional rules">
        Add pricing tiers and minting restrictions on the other tabs, or skip them. See [Configure pricing and rules](#configure-pricing-and-rules).
      </Step>

      <Step title="Review the listing">
        Click **Review**. The summary lists each section with its settings. Click a section to change it, then click **Deploy**.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/activate-review.png" alt="Review screen summarizing Setup, Prices, Reservations, Mint window, Whitelist, Token gated, and Other, each marked OK, with the Deploy button" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Activate and confirm in your wallet">
        The app lists the activation steps:

        1. **Set up subname registry** deploys the registry that holds your name's subnames. You own it.
        2. **Allow Namespace to mint subnames** lets Namespace's subname issuer register and renew subnames in that registry.
        3. **Save activation** saves your pricing, rules, and payment settings.

        Click **Activate** and confirm in your wallet.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/activate-confirm-steps.png" alt="Activation steps Set up subname registry, Allow Namespace to mint subnames, and Save activation, each marked Bundled, with the Activate button" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Finish the activation">
        When all three steps show **Confirmed**, the app shows that your name is activated. Click **Done**. People can now mint subnames of your name.

        <Frame>

    ## Configure pricing and rules

    Every tab after **Setup** is optional, and tabs you skip stay marked **OFF**. Turn a rule on with the toggle at the top of its tab. The **Price check** panel shows which rule applies to a label as you change them.

    ### Prices

    The **Prices** tab has two optional rule types. Each has its own toggle.

    **Label length pricing** sets a yearly price for labels of a specific length. Click **Add length tier**, enter the number of characters, then enter the price. Short labels are scarce, so you might charge $50 for one character, $20 for two, and $10 for three.

    **Character-class pricing** sets a price for labels made only of letters (**Letters only**) or only of numbers (**Numbers only**). For example, letter-only labels could cost $5 and number-only labels $2. Labels that mix letters and numbers match neither class.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/onchain/configure-prices.png" alt="Prices tab with label length pricing at 50, 20, and 10 dollars per year for one, two, and three characters, and character-class pricing at 5 dollars for letters only and 2 dollars for numbers only" />
    </Frame>

    ### Reservations

    Reservations block labels or give them a custom price, and they override every other rule. Add a label, then choose **Blocked** so nobody can mint it, or **Priced** and enter its price.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/onchain/configure-reservations.png" alt="Reservations tab with admin and token blocked and degen priced at 100 dollars" />
    </Frame>

    ### Mint window

    Allow minting only between two dates. Set **Opens at** and **Closes at**, or leave one side empty for an open start or end.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/onchain/configure-mint-window.png" alt="Mint window tab with opening and closing dates set three days apart" />
    </Frame>

    ### Whitelist

    Add wallet addresses under **Allowed addresses**, then pick a mode:

    - **Gate**: only listed wallets can mint.
    - **Free mint**: anyone can mint, and listed wallets pay nothing.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/onchain/configure-whitelist.png" alt="Whitelist tab in Free mint mode with three allowed addresses" />
    </Frame>

    ### Token gated

    Turn on **Token-gated access** to let only wallets that hold specific tokens mint. For each token, pick the **Standard** (ERC721, ERC1155, or ERC20), paste the token **Contract** address, and set **Min. held**, the minimum balance a minter needs. Click **Add token** to add another.

    With more than one token, choose how to combine them under **Combine with**:

    - **All of them**: the minter must hold every listed token.
    - **Any one**: holding one of the listed tokens is enough.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/onchain/configure-token-gated.png" alt="Token gated tab with Any one selected, an ERC20 token requiring at least 200 held, an ERC721 token requiring at least 1, and the Add token button" />
    </Frame>

    ### Other settings

    **Use a custom resolver** registers every subname with the resolver you set, whatever resolver the minting app asks for.

    **Owner permissions** controls what a subname's owner can do after minting. **Default** lets owners set a resolver, create subnames under their subname, and transfer it. **Renew** and **Unregister** are off by default. Choose **Custom** to change these. Unregister permanently destroys the subname and frees the label for anyone to mint, so it's usually left off.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/onchain/configure-other-settings.png" alt="Other settings tab with the custom resolver off and default owner permissions: set resolver, create subnames, and transfer on; renew and unregister off" />
    </Frame>

    ## Mint a subname

    After activation, anyone can mint a subname from your name's page in the Namespace App or through [Namespace search](/user-guide/app/search-register). Minting one yourself is a quick way to check the listing.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Search for a label">
        On the name's page, type a label under **Find your perfect subname**. The app shows whether the subname is available and its price. Click **Mint**.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/mint-search-subname.png" alt="Find your perfect subname search showing happy.onchin.eth is available for free on Ethereum, with the Mint button" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Choose a duration and review the total">
        For an expirable listing, set the duration in years. The dialog shows the price, estimated network fees, and the total.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/mint-subname-dialog.png" alt="Mint dialog for happy.onchin.eth with a 1 year duration, a free price, estimated network fees in ETH, and Mint and Cancel buttons" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add profile records">
        Optionally, open **Complete your profile** to add an avatar, nickname, short bio, social accounts, addresses, a website, or custom records. Click **Save**, then click **Mint** and confirm in your wallet.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/mint-complete-profile.png" alt="Profile editor with an uploaded avatar, nickname, and short bio on the General tab, plus Social, Addresses, Website, and Custom tabs" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Confirm the mint">
        When the transaction confirms, the dialog shows the minted subname, mint price, and transaction fees, with a link to Etherscan.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/onchain/mint-success.png" alt="Minted confirmation for happy.onchin.eth with mint price, transaction fees, total, View Name, and Mint Another" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    The subname resolves like any other ENS name, with the owner, expiry, and records you set.

    ## How ENSv1 listings work

    ENSv1 listings issue subnames on Ethereum (L1) or on an L2 network such as Base or Optimism. Subnames already minted on Base and Optimism keep resolving.

    | Model | Issuance cost | Ownership |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | L1 | Ethereum transaction and mint price | The minted subname is held by its owner |
    | L2 | L2 transaction and mint price | The minted subname is held by its owner |

    ## Edit an ENSv1 listing

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the listing">
        Open **Onchain subnames** > **ENSv1**, connect the owner wallet, and select the activated name.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Click Edit Activation">
        Click **Edit Activation** to change pricing, reservations, deadlines, allowlists, or token-gating rules. Each tab has an **Update** button that saves that tab's changes.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/activate-ens-name-4.png" alt="Prices tab of an ENSv1 listing with a base price in ETH and prices by label length for three- and four-character labels" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Confirm the transaction">
        Confirm the update in your wallet.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Review every transaction carefully. Some ENS fuse changes on wrapped names can't be undone.
    </Tip>

    ### Listing settings

    - **Setup**: currency, treasury address, collection name and symbol, and whether subnames can be burned or rented.
    - **Prices**: the base mint price, prices by label length, and special-name pricing.
    - **Reservations**: block names or give them a custom price.
    - **Deadline**: stop minting after a date.
    - **Whitelist**: let only listed wallets mint, or let them mint for free.
    - **Token Gated**: require minters to hold an ERC-20 or ERC-721 token.

    ## How ENSv1 listings were activated

    For reference, these are the steps owners followed to activate an ENSv1 name.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Onchain section">
        The owner connected their wallet, selected the ENS name, and clicked **Activate Name**.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/activate-ens-name-1.png" alt="Onchain section with an ENS name selected and the Activate Name button" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Choose L1 or L2 issuance">
        **L1 ENS Subnames** issued subnames on Ethereum Mainnet. **L2 ENS Subnames** issued them on a supported L2 picked from a dropdown.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/activate-ens-name-2.png" alt="Listing type selection for L1 and L2 subnames" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Configure the listing">
        The owner set the currency, treasury address, collection name and symbol, and whether subnames could be burned or rented. Then they set the base mint price and any length-based or special-name pricing.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/activate-ens-name-3.png" alt="Listing setup fields for currency, treasury, collection metadata, burning, and renting" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add optional minting rules">
        Reservations blocked names or gave them a custom price. Owners could also add a deadline, restrict minting with an allowlist, or require an ERC-20 or ERC-721 token.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/activate-ens-name-5.png" alt="Reservation settings for blocking names or assigning custom prices" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Activate the listing">
        The owner reviewed the configuration, clicked **Activate**, and confirmed the transaction. On Ethereum Mainnet this could require wrapping the ENS name. L2 listings also needed a registry contract and the Namespace Hybrid Resolver.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Finish the activation">
        After any remaining registry or resolver transactions, the owner clicked **Finish Activation**. The name then showed an **Activated** badge.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/activate-ens-name-11.png" alt="Activated ENS name with its minted subnames" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ## How users mint

    Users mint subnames of an ENSv1 listing through:

    - The name's page at `app.namespace.ninja/<name>.eth`.
    - [Namespace search](/user-guide/app/search-register).

    ENSv1 listings also work with the deprecated [ENS Widget](/user-guide/ens-widget), [Subpages](/user-guide/subpages), and [Mint Manager](/developer-guide/guide/mint-l1-l2-subnames).
