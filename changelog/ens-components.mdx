<Update label="October 28, 2026" description="v3.0.0">
  - Breaking:
    - **Onchain flows run on ENSv2.** `version` on `EnsNameRegistrationForm`, `NameRenewForm`, `EnsRecordsForm`, and `SubnameMintForm` defaults to `2`. Pass `version={1}` to `SubnameMintForm` for parents on an old Namespace listing.
    - **`TransactionPendingScreen` no longer takes a `message` prop.**
  - Added:
    - **`NameRenewForm`**: renew a `.eth` name in one step, paid in USDC or DAI.
    - **`SubnameRenewForm`**: renew a subname at the parent's price, with a warning when the subname would outlive its parent.
    - **`TransactionProgress` and `TransactionModal`**: the confirmation screen, exported for your own transactions.
  - Changed:
    - Registration and renewal re-send the `referrer`, defaulting to Namespace's.
    - `SubnameMintForm` accepts ETH, USDC, or DAI on mainnet.
    - The mint fee estimate uses the effective gas price.
</Update>

<Update label="July 27, 2026" description="v1.5.0">
  - Changed:
    - Updated avatar and header record support to use `@thenamespace/avatar` v2.
    - Externalized the Avatar SDK from the browser bundle.
</Update>

<Update label="July 11, 2026" description="v1.4.0">

</Update>

<Update label="April 30, 2026" description="v1.3.1">
  - Fixed:
    - Stabilized ENS registration fee estimation.
    - Added an RPC fallback for fee estimation.

</Update>

<Update label="April 28, 2026" description="v1.3.0">

</Update>
